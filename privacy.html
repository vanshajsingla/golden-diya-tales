Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers Golden Diya Tales uploader (the "App"), a private tool used only by the owner of the Golden Diya Tales YouTube channel to publish the channel's own videos. The App is not offered to the public, and nobody other than the channel owner signs in to it.

What the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App is allowed to access the owner's YouTube account in order to:

  • upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions;
  • change the privacy status of those videos (for example, private to public) and add them to playlists;
  • delete a video the owner decides not to publish;
  • read the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views and audience retention) to improve future stories.

How data is stored

The sign-in token and analytics numbers are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared, transferred or sent to any other service, and are not used for advertising.

Viewers of the channel

The App does not collect personal information about people who watch Golden Diya Tales videos. Viewing videos on YouTube is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google API Services

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops the App from accessing the account immediately.
